# Privacy policy (excerpt)

*This is a stand-in for the real policy document a team would point the gate at.
It exists so the demo can show the check reading a company's own written
commitments instead of assuming.*

## Deletion

When you delete your account we remove your messages, sessions and profile
within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and it is not a hidden flag.

**One exception.** Messages that violated our rules are retained for up to 12
months for safety purposes, so that people cannot evade detection by sending
something harmful and then deleting it. Retained messages are accessible only
to the safety team, are never used for analytics or product metrics, and are
disclosed in the deletion confirmation screen.

## Analytics

Analytics and product metrics never include accounts that have been deleted or
are pending deletion. Safety review is separate from analytics and is scoped to
the exception above.

## Sessions

Deleting an account revokes all active sessions and tokens immediately, before
any retention window begins.
